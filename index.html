About Nexus Global University

Our journey, mission, and vision for the future

Our Mission

To provide world-class education that fosters innovation, critical thinking, and social responsibility, preparing students to thrive in a rapidly evolving global landscape.

Our Vision

To be a leading global institution recognized for excellence in research, technology-driven learning, and community impact.

Our History

Founded in 2010, Nexus Global University (NGU) began as a small institute of technology in Nairobi. Over the past decade, we have expanded into a comprehensive university with five faculties and over 5,000 students from 20 different countries. Our commitment to quality assurance and student satisfaction remains at the core of everything we do.
